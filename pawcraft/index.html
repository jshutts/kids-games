🐾 Tips

Your doghouse is a safe spot. Bad guys can't grab you inside, and you heal there.

Grown-ups are too tall to fit in tunnels! Dig a tunnel to hide.

Dig in the dirt to find buried bones. Robot dogs drop iron.

Stuck in a hole? Hold jump next to a wall to scramble up!
